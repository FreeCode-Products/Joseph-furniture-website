## Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ Keyword stuffing
- ❌ Hidden text or links
- ❌ Cloaking (showing different content to bots)
- ❌ Duplicate content
- ❌ Purchasing backlinks
- ❌ Private link networks
- ❌ Misleading titles/descriptions
- ❌ Auto-generated content
- ❌ Doorway pages
- ❌ Redirects for deceptive purposes
